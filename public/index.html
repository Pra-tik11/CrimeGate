Features

Everything You Need to Stay Safe

Built with modern security standards to ensure your complaint data is safe and handled efficiently.

End-to-End Secure
JWT authentication + bcrypt encryption protects all user data and sessions from unauthorized access.
Location-Based Assignment
Complaints are automatically routed to the nearest police station and an available officer is assigned.
Real-Time Status Updates
Every time your case status changes, you can instantly view the update with officer remarks.
Evidence File Upload
Attach photos, videos or documents as evidence directly when filing your complaint.
